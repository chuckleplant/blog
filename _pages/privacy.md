This Privacy Policy describes how chuckleplant.com (“the Site”, “I”, or “me”)
collects, uses, and protects personal information for users located in Spain.
By accessing or using the Site, you agree to the practices described below.

## Information I Collect

- **LinkedIn profile data** that you choose to share when connecting your
  account.
- **Name and email address** provided through the Site (for example, when you
  contact me or submit content).

No other personal information is collected, and cookies or analytics are not in
use at this time.

## How I Use Your Information

I only use the data you provide to offer and improve the functionality of the
Site, to respond to your requests, and—when applicable—to provide the specific
service you asked for (such as retrieving LinkedIn data). I do not sell, rent,
or otherwise disclose your personal information to third parties. Data is only
shared back with you, the user who supplied it, unless I am legally required to
do otherwise.

## Data Storage and Location

All personal information is stored in Spain. I retain the data for as long as it
is needed for the purposes described in this policy, or until I decide that it
is no longer necessary. You may request deletion at any time (see “Your Rights”
below).

## Security

I use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the personal
information I store, including limiting access to authorized personnel and
securing API credentials (such as LinkedIn secrets) in protected storage. While
no system can guarantee absolute security, I take all practical steps to keep
your data safe.

## Your Rights

You may:

- Request a copy of the personal information I hold about you.
- Ask me to correct inaccurate data.
- Request deletion of your information.

To exercise any of these rights, email
[sergio.basurco@gmail.com](mailto:sergio.basurco@gmail.com). I will respond in a
reasonable timeframe and honor your request unless I am legally required to keep
certain information.

## Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date
indicates when the latest changes were posted. Significant changes will be
communicated through the Site or via email when appropriate.
